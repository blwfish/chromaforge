### Removing a photo from a Hard Drive collection removes only what it published

- Taking a photo out of a Hard Drive published collection deleted, for good, every `.xmp` file with the same name in the collection's folder, even an original's own sidecar. Now only the files the collection wrote go (the render, and the sidecar of an Original export), and they go to the system trash.
- A Hard Drive collection whose folder holds originals never writes over one of them or its sidecar: that photo fails to publish and says why.
