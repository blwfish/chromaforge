## Metadata
- **All**, **No camera**, **Copyright**, **Copyright & Contact**, **None**.
- **Remove location info** strips GPS and every place name: sublocation, city, state, country and country code. An original copied byte for byte is refused when it embeds any of them (in Exif, XMP, including the IPTC Extension's Location Created and Location Shown, or IPTC-IIM), or when its metadata cannot be fully checked (a cut-short PNG or WebP, an over-limit XMP packet), with JPEG or TIFF offered instead. A video clip's original is always refused under it: export the clip in another format, which writes a new movie.

## Watermarking
- **Watermark** checkbox with a live preview on the photo.
- **Style**: **Text** or **Graphic** (an image file).
- **Text direction**: **Horizontal text** or **Vertical text** (columns run down, a newline starts the next column to the left; built for Japanese).
- **Position**: nine anchors in a 3 × 3 grid laid out like the corners, edges and centre they pick; **Rotate** 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°.
- **Size** (% of short edge), **Opacity**, **Horizontal / Vertical inset**, **Shadow**.
- 🟡 Complex scripts: Thai mark positioning is incomplete and Arabic is not supported in the watermark. Japanese and Chinese need the bundled CJK fonts.
- Vertical text keeps Latin upright; no rotated runs, tate-chu-yoko or ruby.
